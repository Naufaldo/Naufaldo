# Hi there 👋 I'm Naufaldo

**M.Sc. Mechatronics Engineer · Cold Storage & Automation Engineer · Robotics Researcher · Director**

I am an engineer and researcher working at the intersection of **industrial refrigeration, automation, robotics, software, and intelligent control systems**.

I currently lead **CV Dingin Lestari Teknik**, a Bandung-based engineering company specializing in **cold storage and refrigeration systems**. Alongside industrial engineering, I continue to develop software and conduct research in **multi-agent robotics, formation control, autonomous navigation, and intelligent control**.

I hold a **Master of Science in Mechatronics Engineering from National Taipei University of Technology (Taipei Tech), Taiwan**, with research focused on intelligent control and multi-agent mobile robotics.
